## The problem

Gyms are notoriously bad places for connectivity, and most fitness apps either stop working offline or quietly sell your health data. IronEcho had to be **usable with zero signal** and **owned entirely by its user**.

## What I built

I designed the backend and the synchronisation engine.

- **Local-first.** The mobile client writes to Realm instantly; every change is recorded and queued.
- **Sync & conflict resolution.** When connectivity returns, queued operations are replayed against the API. The API validates every change with Joi, and the sync engine resolves conflicts last-write-wins by comparing modification timestamps, so data stays consistent across devices.
- **Real-time updates.** Socket.io pushes changes to other connected sessions.
- **Security.** Stateless JWTs with separate access and refresh tokens, Helmet, rate limiting and Joi validation on every input.

## Running it

MongoDB data models cover workouts, sets, body metrics and analytics. The whole backend ships as a **Docker Compose** stack — a single command brings it up on a VPS or on a Raspberry Pi in a home lab.

## What I'd improve

Replace last-write-wins with field-level merges (or CRDTs) for richer concurrent edits, and add end-to-end tests that simulate flaky networks.
